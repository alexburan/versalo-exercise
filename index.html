Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Write to us using the form below.

Do I need a developer to install Versalo?

No. You paste one line of code into your site, or install our plugin. It takes about five minutes.

Can I edit the AI translations?

Yes. Every sentence can be edited in the visual editor, and your edits are never overwritten.

Will translated pages show up in Google?

Yes. Each language gets its own URL with translated titles, descriptions and hreflang tags.

What happens when my trial ends?

You choose a plan, or your site simply returns to its original language. Nothing is charged automatically.
